FAQ

Your Questions.
Answered.

01

What is an Open Banking payment?

Your customer pays straight from their bank account instead of with a card. They choose their bank, approve the payment in their banking app, and are redirected back to your store.

02

How does my customer pay?

At checkout they choose Pay by bank, pick their bank from 350+ banks across the EEA and the UK, and approve the payment in their banking app. No card details are entered.

03

How fast do I receive my money?

Instantly. Once a customer pays, the funds reach your AvakPay merchant balance right away, and you can withdraw them instantly to your business account.

04

Is it secure?

Yes. Customers approve every payment in their own banking app, and no card details are entered or shared. Encryption, fraud prevention, and monitoring protect every transaction.

05

Which banks and countries are covered?

AvakPay connects to 350+ banks across the EEA and the UK.
